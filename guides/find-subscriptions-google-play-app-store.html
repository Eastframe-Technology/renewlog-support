How to find what you are subscribed to on Google Play and the App Store

Each store keeps a list of what it bills you for. It takes less than a minute to open. It only shows subscriptions billed through that store, so a charge you paid to a website will not be there.

On Google Play

On an Android phone:

  1. Open the Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions.
  4. Tap Subscriptions.

You can also open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions in a browser while signed in to the same Google account.

Tap a subscription to see its price and next payment date, change the payment method or cancel.

Have more than one Google account? The list belongs to one account at a time. Tap your profile picture, switch account and look again. This is a common reason for a subscription to seem missing.

In the Apple App Store

On an iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.

Or open the App Store app, tap your picture at the top right, then tap Subscriptions. There is also a direct link: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

The page lists active subscriptions and recently expired ones. Tap one to see its renewal date or cancel. To stop a free trial from renewing, Apple asks you to cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Used more than one Apple Account? Sign in with the other one and check there too. Apple's list only shows the account you are signed in to.

Store menus change from time to time. If a label differs on your phone, look for "subscriptions" in the account or profile area.

Why a charge may not be listed

These lists only include what the store itself bills. A subscription will not appear if:

  • You signed up on the service's website, even if you use it through its app.
  • You pay through a phone carrier, a TV provider or a payment app.
  • Someone else in your family pays for it from their own account.
  • You used a different Google or Apple account.

So if a service charges you and the store list does not show it, go to the service's website, sign in and look under account or billing.

Reading your card statement

Store purchases are labelled by the store, not always by the app, which makes them hard to recognise.

  • Apple charges usually appear as APPLE.COM/BILL.
  • Google Play charges usually start with GOOGLE, often followed by the name of the service.

If you see one and do not recognise it, check the subscription list first, then your purchase history in the same store.

What to do with what you find

  1. Write each one down with the price, the billing period and the next date.
  2. Add them up. The subscription cost calculator handles a mix of weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly plans.
  3. Cancel what you do not use. Cancelling normally leaves you with access until the end of the period you already paid for. A cancelled subscription may still be listed until then.
  4. Check your statements too. This guide covers the places the stores cannot show you.

Remember that deleting an app does not stop its subscription. On either store, you cancel from the list above.
