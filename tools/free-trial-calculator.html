How it works

  • End date. Start date plus the trial length on the calendar. Days and weeks count calendar days. Months and years keep the same day number and stop at the end of a short month.
  • Safe last day to cancel. The calendar day before the end date.
  • Reminder. The number of calendar days you pick before the end date, at 9:00 your time. That is the same rule the RenewLog app uses for its trial warning.

The sums use dates only, never hours. A local day can be 23 or 25 hours long when the clocks change, so adding 24 hours at a time can land on the wrong day.

Worked example

You start a 7-day trial on Sunday, 1 November 2026, and ask for a reminder 3 days before.

Trial ends, first charge dueSunday, 8 November 2026
Safe last day to cancelSaturday, 7 November 2026
ReminderThursday, 5 November 2026, 9:00

In the US the clocks go back on 1 November 2026. The answer does not change.

Limits

  • Services decide their own terms. Your receipt or the store's subscription page is the final word on the date.
  • The exact time of the charge is not something a calculator can know. That is why the safe day is a day early.
  • This page does not remind you by itself. Add the calendar event, or set your own alarm.

Frequently asked

Does day one count?

Count from the day you signed up as day zero. A 7-day trial started on 1 November usually ends on 8 November, at about the same time of day you started. Your receipt or the store's subscription page shows the exact date, and that date wins over any calculator.

Why tell me to cancel a day early?

The charge can happen at any time on the end date, and the service may run on a different time zone from yours. The App Store also asks you to cancel at least 24 hours before a trial ends. One day early clears all of that.

What does "1 month" mean if I start on 31 January?

It ends on the last day of February: the 28th, or the 29th in a leap year. Months keep the same day number where they can and stop at the end of a short month, like most billing systems. RenewLog works the same way.

Does daylight saving change the date?

No. This page works with calendar dates, not hours, so the clock change does not move the answer. Adding 24 hours at a time is what goes wrong: in the US on 1 November 2026, 24 hours after midnight is still 1 November.

What is in the calendar file?

One event at 9:00 on your reminder date with an alert. It uses local time, so it rings at 9:00 on that date wherever you are. The file is made in your browser. Open it with your calendar app to add the event.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The dates and the service name stay in this browser tab.
