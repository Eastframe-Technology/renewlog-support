Subscription cost calculator

List what you pay, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, and see the total per month and per year. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Keeping this list? RenewLog for Android holds it on your phone and reminds you before each charge. No bank login.

How it works

Every subscription is turned into an average cost per month, then the months are added up and multiplied by 12.

BilledCost per month
Weeklyprice × 365.2425 ÷ 7 ÷ 12
Monthlyprice
Quarterlyprice ÷ 3
Yearlyprice ÷ 12

The 365.2425 is the average length of a year, leap years included. This is the same formula the RenewLog app uses, so the two give the same totals.

Limits

  • These are averages. A given month can cost more than its average, because a yearly charge lands all at once.
  • Prices are taken as you type them. Price rises, discounts and intro offers are not modelled.
  • It is arithmetic, not financial advice.

Frequently asked

Why is a weekly subscription not just price times 52?

A year is 365.2425 days on average, which is about 52.18 weeks. Multiplying by 52 leaves out one or two charges a year. This calculator uses the average, the same as the RenewLog app.

Does it convert currencies?

No. Each currency gets its own total. Converting would need exchange rates from the internet, and this page never goes online after it loads.

Is my list saved or sent anywhere?

No. The numbers stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close it. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored or logged.

What about a subscription billed every 6 months?

Enter twice the price and choose Yearly. A 6-month plan at 30 is 60 a year, so type 60. The totals come out right.

Does it include tax?

Only if the price you type does. Use the amount you actually see on your statement.
